# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of identity, background,
experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking people.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Help keep issues and reviews focused on improving the project.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[thiago.hirai@gmail.com](mailto:thiago.hirai@gmail.com) with the subject
`Spec Butler conduct report`. Do not use a public issue when a report
identifies a target or contains sensitive details. Maintainers may remove
content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect
the community.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical, investigate in
good faith, and avoid conflicts of interest. Retaliation against someone who
reports a concern is itself a violation of this policy.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone is officially
representing the project elsewhere.
